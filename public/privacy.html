Privacy Policy

By Scanner.cam Team · Updated 16 Oct 2026

The short version

Scanner.cam processes everything on your device. We never receive, store, or see your scanned documents or photos — there is no server endpoint that accepts them, so there is nothing to leak, sell, or subpoena.

What we collect

Your documents: never uploaded, never stored, never transmitted. Accounts: there are none, so we hold no names, emails, or passwords. Analytics: we use privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics (Google Analytics) to understand overall usage patterns, such as which pages are visited. This does not include document contents or images.

Cookies

The scanner may use browser storage (IndexedDB/localStorage) on your own device to recover an interrupted scan session. This data stays in your browser and is never sent to us. Analytics cookies follow Google's policies; you can block them in your browser settings.

Third parties

We do not sell personal data. We do not share document contents with anyone, because we never have them. Aggregate, non-identifying analytics may be processed by Google Analytics under their terms.

Children

Scanner.cam is a general-audience tool with no accounts and no data collection from documents. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the updated date below. Last updated: 12 Oct 2026.

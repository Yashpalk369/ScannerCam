Get in touch

Questions, bug reports, feature requests, or press inquiries — we'd like to hear from you.

How to reach us

Email is the fastest route. Use the contact address published on our official channels, and include "Scanner.cam" in the subject line so your message gets routed correctly. For bug reports, please mention your device, browser version, and what you were doing when the problem occurred — screenshots help.

Response time

We're a small independent team and read everything, though replies may take a few days during busy periods.

Security issues

If you believe you've found a security problem, please report it privately rather than posting it publicly, and give us reasonable time to address it before disclosure.

When CamScanner still makes sense

If you need advanced OCR, cloud sync across many devices, or team collaboration features, a paid app can be worth it. For everyone else scanning receipts, contracts, homework, or IDs, the free tier limits are the pain point.

What "free" should mean

  • No watermark — your documents shouldn't advertise someone else's app.
  • No forced account — scanning a receipt shouldn't require an email address.
  • No cloud upload — sensitive documents stay on your device.
  • No page limits — multi-page PDFs without a paywall.

Scanner.cam hits all four: it runs entirely in your browser using on-device computer vision (perspective correction, shadow removal), and exports clean PDFs with zero uploads.

The bottom line

For occasional and even heavy personal scanning, a free web scanner covers everything the paid apps do for basic digitizing — without the watermark, the subscription, or the privacy trade-off.

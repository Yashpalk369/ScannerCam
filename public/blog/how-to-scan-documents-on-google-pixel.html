How to Scan Documents on a Google Pixel With Google Camera

By Scanner.cam Team · Updated 20 Oct 2026 · 4 min read
Quick answer

Your Google Pixel phone can scan documents without installing any scanner app. The stock Google Camera app detects pages in the viewfinder and offers a one-tap "Scan document" prompt — with edge detection, perspective correction, and save-as-PDF built in.

What the built-in Pixel scanner can do

  • Automatic document detection. Point the Camera app at a page and hold steady — a "Scan document" prompt appears over the viewfinder when a document is recognized.
  • One-tap scan with cropping. Tap the prompt and you get a cropping interface to fine-tune the page edges, then share the result as an image or PDF.
  • Text detection and extraction. Google Lens powers text recognition in the viewfinder — it detects printed text, offers copy in your language or translate for foreign text, plus "Crop document" actions on photos you already took.
  • Saved right where you expect. Scans save to your Photos library, so they're easy to find, share to Drive or Gmail, or attach to an email.
  • Everything runs on the phone. No sign-up, no watermark, no third-party cloud upload involved in the capture itself.

Step-by-step: scan with the Google Camera app

  1. Open the Camera app and make sure you're in Photo mode.
  2. Lay the document flat in good light and point the camera at it, framing all four corners.
  3. Hold relatively still and wait for the "Scan document" prompt to appear on screen.
  4. Tap Scan document.
  5. Adjust the corner handles in the cropping interface if any edge was missed.
  6. Save or share — choose image or PDF, then send it to Drive, Gmail, or wherever it needs to go.

Scan documents from Google Photos too

Already photographed a document? You don't need to reshoot it. In the Google Photos app, open the Library tab and use the Scan document option to capture a fresh scan of a page — or open an existing photo of a document and tap the Lens icon to extract text, identify objects, or apply the suggested "Crop document" cleanup. This is handy for tidying up photos taken before you knew the scanner was there.

Get cleaner results from the built-in scanner

  • Light it evenly. Daylight near a window keeps text contrast high and avoids shadows that confuse edge detection.
  • Contrast the background. Detection locks on fastest when the page edges stand out — a white page on a dark desk works best.
  • Fill the frame. Get close so the document takes up most of the viewfinder; the detection and the final resolution both improve.
  • Hold still for a beat. The "Scan document" prompt appears once the preview stabilizes — waving the phone around resets the detection.
  • Retake beats repair. If text is blurred or a shadow crosses the page, retake the scan rather than trying to fix it later.

Turn Pixel scans into a clean multi-page PDF

The Camera app's scanner handles single pages well, but multi-page documents come out as separate files. To finish properly:

  1. For a quick PDF: save the scan as a PDF and compile pages in Google Drive — or use the Photos document scan for each page.
  2. For a private, watermark-free PDF: open Scanner.cam in your Pixel's browser, capture or upload your pages, crop and clean them, then export one consolidated PDF. Nothing uploads — the whole process runs on your phone, so sensitive paperwork stays private.
  3. Name and file it: "2026-10-18-lease-agreement.pdf" is findable; "scan-004.pdf" is not.

FAQ

Do I need an app to scan documents on a Pixel?
No. The stock Google Camera app on Pixel phones includes document detection — point it at a page, tap the "Scan document" prompt, and save as an image or PDF. Google Photos also offers document scanning and Lens-powered cleanup on photos you already took.

Where do Pixel document scans save?
To your Google Photos library, from where you can share them to Drive, Gmail, or any other app. Scans are saved as images or PDFs depending on the option you choose.

Why isn't the "Scan document" prompt appearing?
Document detection needs a flat, well-lit page with strong contrast against the background, and the phone held steady for a moment. Dark text on white paper over a dark surface works best; make sure all four corners are in the frame.

Is scanning documents with the Pixel Camera private?
The capture and edge detection happen on the phone itself — nothing is uploaded by the Camera app. Just be mindful of where you back up or share the scans afterwards, since Photos may sync them to your Google account.

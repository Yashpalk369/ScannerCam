How to Scan Documents on iPhone Without Downloading an App

You don't need the App Store to scan a document on your iPhone. Your Safari browser is already a capable scanner — you just need a web tool that uses the camera, straightens the page, and exports a PDF. Here's how to do it in under two minutes, completely free.

Option 1: Use a free online scanner (fastest)

  1. Open Safari and go to Scanner.cam.
  2. Tap Scan Camera and allow camera access when prompted.
  3. Frame your document inside the guides and capture the photo.
  4. Drag the four corner handles to the paper edges — the 2.5x magnifying loupe helps you place them precisely.
  5. Choose Magic Color to remove shadows or Clean B/W for crisp text.
  6. Tap Export to download a clean, watermark-free PDF.

Everything runs locally in Safari — your document never uploads to a server, which matters for contracts, IDs, and medical papers.

Option 2: The built-in Notes app

iPhone's Notes app can scan: open a note, tap the camera icon, choose "Scan Documents." It works, but the output often needs cleanup and sharing options are limited compared to a dedicated scanner.

Why skip the App Store?

  • No storage used — scanner apps are often 100MB+.
  • No subscriptions — most scanning apps charge $5–10/month for watermark-free PDFs.
  • No account required — nothing to sign up for, nothing tracking you.
  • Better privacy — browser-based scanning keeps files on your device.

Tips for a clean iPhone scan

  • Place the document on a contrasting background (dark desk for white paper).
  • Use even, indirect light to avoid harsh shadows.
  • Hold the phone parallel to the page, not at an angle.
  • For multi-page documents, capture every page first, then export once as a single PDF.

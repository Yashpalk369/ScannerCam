How to Scan Homework Assignments to Submit Online

By Scanner.cam Editorial Team · Updated 7 Nov 2026 · 4 min read
Quick answer

To scan homework assignments for online submission, photograph each page in good light, straighten and crop it, and combine the pages into a single PDF in the right order. Name the file with your name and the assignment title, then upload it to Google Classroom, Teams, or your school portal before the deadline.

Most homework now ends as a file upload, not a piece of paper handed in. The scan is the assignment as far as your teacher is concerned — if a page is upside down, blurry, or missing, that is what gets marked. A good submission scan takes two minutes longer than a rushed photo, and it is the difference between full marks for presentation and a message asking you to resubmit.

Scan the assignment properly

  1. Flatten the pages first. Homework pages curl, fold, and pick up creases in a school bag. Put them under a heavy book for a few minutes, or smooth them flat on a table before you shoot.
  2. Use bright, even light. Daylight near a window, or a desk lamp from the side. Shadows across handwritten answers are the most common reason a teacher cannot read a submission.
  3. Photograph every page, including the back. Check both sides — answers written on the back of a sheet are the easiest pages to forget.
  4. Check each page at full zoom. Your handwriting needs to be readable at 100 percent. If a line is soft, re-shoot that page before moving on.
  5. Merge into one PDF in the right order. Page 1 first, then page 2, and so on. A single PDF is what teachers expect — a folder of separate photos is not a submission.

Name the file the way your teacher expects

Before you upload, rename the file. A clear name is your name, the class or subject, and the assignment title — something like "Ayesha-Khan-Maths-Homework-3.pdf" rather than "scan001.pdf". Many teachers download dozens of files into one folder, and a file named after the student and the assignment is the one that gets found and marked first. If your teacher gave a naming format, follow it exactly. If your assignment includes handwritten workings, the same clean-capture steps in how to scan handwritten notes to PDF apply — the goal is the same: every word readable, every page in order.

Before you hit submit

  • Open the finished PDF and read it like your teacher will. Scroll through every page. Check the order, the orientation, and that no page is cut off at the edge.
  • Check the file size. School portals often cap uploads at a few megabytes. If your PDF is too large, re-export it at a lower quality rather than sending it in pieces.
  • Confirm the right file is attached. It is easy to attach last week's homework by mistake. Check the file name in the upload box before submitting.
  • Submit early when you can. Uploads fail, portals go down, and files get rejected for size. Submitting the night before beats submitting two minutes before the deadline.
  • Keep a copy. Save the submitted PDF on your phone or in Drive. If the portal loses it, you can resend it in seconds.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Sending photos instead of a PDF. Photos arrive sideways, out of order, and one at a time. A single PDF is the standard for a reason.
  • Missing the back of a page. Always check both sides. Teachers notice a missing page before they notice anything else.
  • Scanning in a dark room. A dim photo of neat handwriting is still unreadable. Light matters more than camera quality.
  • Leaving the file named "scan.pdf". Your teacher has thirty files called that. Name yours properly.
  • Submitting without opening the PDF. The one time you skip the check is the time a page is upside down.

Frequently asked questions

What file format should homework be submitted in?
PDF, in almost every case. It keeps the pages in order, looks the same on every device, and is what Google Classroom, Teams, and school portals accept.

Can I scan homework with just my phone?
Yes — a phone camera and a free browser scanner are all you need. Photograph each page, straighten and crop it, and merge the pages into one PDF.

How do I scan both sides of a homework page?
Photograph the front, flip the page, photograph the back, and keep shooting in order. Merge the images in the same sequence so the PDF reads correctly.

Why did my teacher ask me to resubmit my homework?
Usually because a page was missing, unreadable, or out of order. Open your submitted PDF and check it page by page — the problem is normally visible in seconds.

What should I name my homework file?
Your name, the subject, and the assignment title — for example "Ayesha-Khan-Maths-Homework-3.pdf". Follow your teacher's naming format exactly if they gave one.

Why you need an alternative now

Remove.bg has announced that its standalone website will no longer be available from 1 December 2026 at 9:00am CET. Consumers are pointed to Canva, where background removal sits inside paid plans, and self-serve API users are pointed to Leonardo.Ai. Existing enterprise API contracts continue, and unused pay-as-you-go credits expire on the morning of 1 December. If your everyday workflow is upload a photo, get a transparent PNG, and download it — with no subscription — that workflow needs a new home before December. Scanner.cam is not affiliated with or endorsed by remove.bg; it is simply a free tool that does the same core job, privately, in your browser.

How to remove a background free in your browser

  1. Open Scanner.cam's background remover in any modern browser — phone or desktop, no app to install.
  2. Tap Load model. This downloads the 4.4 MB removal model once; your browser caches it, so later visits skip the download.
  3. Choose a photo — JPG, PNG, or WebP — from your device.
  4. Tap Remove background. In our browser test, a portrait photo took about 4 seconds and a product photo about 3 seconds from tap to result; slower phones take a little longer.
  5. Tap Download transparent PNG and save the cutout.

What it handles well — and where it is rougher

The tool is at its best on product photos and portraits with clear edges: a mug photographed against a busy market background cut out cleanly in testing, and a street portrait separated from its background without losing the subject. It is rougher where premium tools earn their money — hair, fur, wispy edges, glass, and low-contrast boundaries can look softer, because the on-device model is 4.4 MB where premium cloud models are far larger. That is the honest trade-off: a small model that runs on phones, privately and free, versus a large model that needs a server and a subscription. For the best results, use a well-lit photo with contrast between your subject and its background, and avoid fine hair against a busy backdrop for the shots that matter most.

Privacy: your photo never leaves your device

Most online removers work by uploading your photo to a server, processing it there, and sending the result back — which means a copy of your photo sits on someone else's infrastructure under their retention policy. Scanner.cam's remover works the other way round: the model downloads to your browser and the removal runs on your device with WebAssembly. Your photo is never transmitted, never stored on a server, and never seen by anyone but you. The first model download needs an internet connection, and the tool page itself loads from the web, but the photo processing is entirely local. For product shots, profile pictures, and marketplace listings, that privacy is free — it is the default, not a paid tier.

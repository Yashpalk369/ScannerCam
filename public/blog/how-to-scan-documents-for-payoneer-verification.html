How to Scan Documents for Payoneer Verification

By Scanner.cam Team · Updated 2 Nov 2026 · 4 min read
Quick answer

To scan documents for Payoneer verification, prepare a clear colour scan of your unexpired government photo ID showing both sides, plus proof of address if asked, then upload them through Payoneer's Verification Center. Payoneer says scanned images are preferable to photos, and the ID must be complete, in colour, and free of obstructions.

Getting the scan right first time is the fastest route to approval, because poor document quality is the most common cause of delays.

Payoneer is a regulated payment service, so verification is not a formality: it has to satisfy identity, address, and banking checks before your account can receive payments. The documents it asks for depend on your country and account type, but the pattern is consistent — a government photo ID first, then supporting documents as needed. This guide follows Payoneer's own published document guidance, so you can prepare exactly what its reviewers expect.

What Payoneer asks for

Start with a government-issued photo ID: a national ID card, passport, or driving licence. It must be valid and unexpired, and you must upload both sides of a card-style ID; for a passport, the photo page is the key page. Payoneer also commonly asks for proof of address — a utility bill, bank statement, or similar official document showing your name and address — and a bank verification document confirming the account you want payouts sent to.

Check your own Verification Center before you scan, because the requested list varies by country and by whether your account is personal or business. Business accounts may need company registration documents too. Scan only what is requested, but scan all of it: a missing document holds up the whole review just as surely as a blurry one.

How to scan an ID Payoneer will accept

Open Scanner.cam in your browser and scan on your device, so your ID never passes through a third-party scanner's server. Lay the ID flat on a plain, dark surface in even natural light, hold the phone directly above it, and capture the full document with all four corners visible. Payoneer is explicit on the details: the image must be clear with no obstructions, a colour copy is preferable to black and white, and if you photograph instead of scanning, the picture must be straight and the flash must not cover any information.

Scan both sides of a card as separate pages or files, and check each at full zoom: photo sharp, all text readable, expiry date visible, no glare on laminated surfaces, no fingers at the edges. For proof of address, scan the full document, not a cropped section — the reviewer needs your name, your address, and the issuing organisation's details on the same page. Make sure the name and address on every document match your Payoneer profile exactly; even small mismatches, such as an initial instead of a full name, can trigger a request for more documents.

Upload through the Verification Center

Sign in to Payoneer, open Settings, then the Verification Center, and upload each document against the request it belongs to. Keep file sizes sensible — a high-resolution phone photo can run to several megabytes, and compressing a scan that is too large is better than cropping content. After uploading, check the status in the same place; Payoneer reviews typically take a few business days, and the Verification Center will show if anything further is needed.

If a document is rejected, read the reason before resubmitting. A rejection for quality means rescan in better light with a steadier hand; a rejection for mismatch means check the names and addresses on your profile against the documents, not the photo quality. Do not submit the same file again unchanged and hope — that just restarts the same clock. And submit documents only through the Verification Center: Payoneer does not verify accounts through links sent by email or chat, and any such request should be treated as phishing.

Mistakes that delay Payoneer approval

  • Expired ID: rejected automatically, however good the scan. Check the expiry date before you start.
  • One side only: card IDs need front and back. A front-only upload is an incomplete submission.
  • Glare and fingers: flash reflections and fingers over text are the two most avoidable quality failures.
  • Black-and-white copies: colour is preferred, and colour reproduces security features and photos faithfully.
  • Name mismatches: your Payoneer profile name must match your ID exactly.
  • Cropped proofs: a proof of address must show the full document, including the issuer's details and date.

Frequently asked questions

Does Payoneer prefer scans or photos of my ID?
Scans. Payoneer's own guidance says scanned images of your ID are preferable to pictures. If you do photograph it, keep the picture straight, in colour, and free of flash glare.

Do I need both sides of my ID for Payoneer?
Yes, for card-style IDs. Upload copies of both sides. For a passport, the photo page is the essential page.

What counts as proof of address for Payoneer?
Typically a utility bill, bank statement, or official letter showing your full name and residential address, matching your Payoneer profile. The accepted list can vary by country.

Why was my Payoneer document rejected?
Usually poor image quality, an expired ID, a missing side, or a name or address mismatch. Read the rejection reason and fix that specific issue before resubmitting.

How long does Payoneer verification take?
Review commonly takes a few business days, and can take longer if additional documents are requested. Track progress in the Verification Center rather than re-uploading.

CamScanner Web Alternative: Everything You Can Do Without the App

By Scanner.cam Editorial Team · Updated 11 Nov 2026 · 6 min read
Quick answer

CamScanner's web version is a companion to its mobile app, not a replacement: you sign in at camscanner.com to view, edit, and share documents you already scanned on your phone, while the app remains the place where new pages are captured. If you want to scan entirely in a browser, with no app and no account, use a browser scanner instead: it covers the capture step the CamScanner website does not.

What the CamScanner website actually is

CamScanner describes its cross-platform sync in its Play Store listing this way: sign up, then sign in on any smartphone, tablet or computer at camscanner.com to view, edit and share your documents. That sentence defines the website's role. It is the place your scanned documents live after the app captures them — a viewer, editor and sharing hub for an account's library, not a camera scanner in its own right.

Two consequences follow. First, the website needs an account: sync is listed as a registrants-only feature, so without signing up there is no library to open on the web. Second, your documents live in CamScanner's cloud, which is what makes them reachable from any computer — and also what means they are stored on a vendor's servers rather than only on your device.

Feature by feature: web vs app

  • Capturing new pages. App: yes — the phone camera is the core of the product, with auto-crop and enhancement. Web: no — the website works with documents already in your account; capturing new pages is the app's job. A browser scanner like Scanner.cam is the no-app substitute here: it captures with your phone's browser camera instead.
  • Viewing and organising documents. App: yes, plus tags and search across your library. Web: yes — view, edit and share synced documents from any computer after signing in.
  • Editing and annotating. App: full annotation tools, plus custom watermarks. Web: edit synced documents, per CamScanner's own description. For a browser-only workflow, edits happen before export (crop, rotate, reorder) rather than as saved annotations on a cloud copy.
  • Multi-page PDFs. App: yes. Web: batch download of PDFs through the web app is listed as a premium feature, not a free one.
  • OCR text extraction. App: premium only — converting images to text, Word or Excel requires the subscription. Web: the same premium boundary applies; there is no free OCR tier on the website.
  • Cloud sync across devices. App and web together: yes, for registered users, with 10 GB of extra cloud space on premium. A no-account browser scanner deliberately does not do this — your PDF downloads to your device and you back it up yourself.
  • Sharing and collaboration. App and web: share PDFs or JPEGs, or send a document download link; up to 10 collaborators per document on free, 40 more on premium, and password-protected, expiring secured links on premium.
  • E-signature. Premium only, on the app.
  • Fax and printing. App only: fax to over 30 countries and AirPrint from the app. The website is not a fax or print route.
  • Watermark and ads. The free version is ad-supported and its scans carry a watermark; removing ads and watermarks is a premium feature. A browser scanner like Scanner.cam produces watermark-free scans with no subscription.
  • Price. CamScanner premium is listed at $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year on its Play Store page, auto-renewing unless cancelled. The browser workflow in this guide costs nothing at any step.

What the web version does not cover

If you do not have the app installed, the CamScanner website on its own leaves three gaps: you cannot capture a new document with your computer or phone camera through it; you cannot use OCR or batch PDF download without paying; and you cannot use it at all without an account. Those gaps are exactly where a browser scanner fits: open Scanner.cam, capture pages with the phone browser, and download a watermark-free PDF with no account — then, if you need text extraction, run the PDF through a free on-device OCR tool rather than a subscription.

The no-app workflow, start to finish

  1. Open Scanner.cam in your phone's browser and allow camera access.
  2. Photograph each page flat, in even light, with the whole page in frame.
  3. Check every thumbnail, retake the bad ones, reorder, and rotate sideways pages.
  4. Download one multi-page PDF and check it before sending.
  5. Back it up yourself: email it to yourself or save it to your cloud drive, since no account is keeping a copy for you.
  6. Need searchable text? Run the finished PDF through a free browser OCR tool that processes on your device, and keep the searchable copy alongside the original scan.

Which one should you use?

Use the CamScanner website when you already use the CamScanner app and need your scanned library on a computer — that is what it is built for, and it does that job well once you are signed in. Use a browser scanner when you want to scan without installing anything, without an account, without a watermark, and without your documents sitting in a vendor's cloud. Use the app (free or premium) when you scan daily, need OCR and batch downloads regularly, or collaborate on documents with a team — and check the premium price against how often you actually use those features before subscribing.

Frequently asked questions

What can you do on the CamScanner website?
After signing in at camscanner.com, you can view, edit and share documents you already scanned with the CamScanner app, from any computer. It is a companion to the app, not a standalone camera scanner.

Can I scan a document on the CamScanner website without the app?
No — capturing new pages is the mobile app's job. The website works with documents already in your account. To scan without any app, use a browser scanner like Scanner.cam instead.

Is CamScanner web free?
Viewing and sharing synced documents is part of a registered account, but batch PDF download through the web app and OCR are premium features, and free scans carry a watermark.

Do I need an account for the CamScanner website?
Yes. Sync is a registrants-only feature, so without an account there is no document library to open on the web.

What is the best alternative to the CamScanner app on the web?
For scanning without installing anything, a browser scanner like Scanner.cam covers capture, clean-up and PDF export with no account and no watermark. It does not, however, keep a synced cloud library — you back up the PDF yourself.

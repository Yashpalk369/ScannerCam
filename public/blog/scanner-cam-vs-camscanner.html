Scanner.cam vs CamScanner: Honest Head-to-Head

By Scanner.cam Team · Updated 27 Oct 2026 · 6 min read
Quick answer

CamScanner is one of the best-known scanner apps in the world, and Scanner.cam is a free scanner that runs entirely in your browser. They solve the same problem — turning paper into a clean PDF — but they do it in very different ways: one is an installed app with accounts, cloud storage, and a paid tier; the other is a web page you open, use, and close.

This guide compares them honestly, including where CamScanner wins, so you can pick the right tool for the job.

Important context first: Scanner.cam is an independent project. It is not CamScanner, it is not affiliated with CamScanner or its developer INTSIG, and it is not endorsed by them. Any comparison below describes two separate products.

The short answer

If you scan occasionally and want a clean PDF with no watermark, no account, and nothing to install, Scanner.cam in your browser is the simpler choice. If you scan every day, need OCR text export, cloud sync across devices, and advanced editing, CamScanner's feature set — free or paid — may be worth the app install and the account. The rest of this guide breaks down why.

At a glance

  • Scanner.cam: free, browser-based, no install, no account, no watermark, documents processed on your device.
  • CamScanner: free app for iOS and Android with ads and a watermark on free-tier exports; a paid Premium subscription (around $4.99 a month, depending on plan and region) removes ads and the watermark and unlocks OCR, ID mode, e-signatures, and more cloud space.
  • The core difference: Scanner.cam trades features for simplicity and privacy; CamScanner trades simplicity for a deep feature set and cross-device cloud.

Cost and watermark

Scanner.cam is free with no watermark on exports — there is no paid tier. CamScanner's free tier stamps exported pages with a "Scanned by CamScanner" watermark and shows ads; removing the watermark requires the Premium subscription. If every document you send out needs to look professional — a contract, a job application, a mortgage pack — a watermark is a real cost, even on a "free" app. That single difference is the reason many people look for a CamScanner alternative in the first place.

Install, account, and setup

Scanner.cam needs nothing: open scanner.cam in any browser, allow camera access, and scan. There is no app to download, no account to create, and no setup step. CamScanner requires an app install from the App Store or Google Play and an account for cloud sync and most features. That install-and-signup friction matters most when you need one scan right now — on someone else's phone, on a shared computer, or on a device with no storage left.

The flip side is convenience over time. Once CamScanner is installed and signed in, your scans live in its cloud and follow you across devices. Scanner.cam keeps everything on the device you scanned with; there is no cloud library to open on another phone.

Scan quality and features

Both tools do the fundamentals well: automatic edge detection, perspective correction, filters, and multi-page PDF export. Where they differ is the extras:

  • OCR (text recognition): CamScanner offers OCR to convert scans into searchable text or Word and Excel files, with fuller OCR behind the Premium tier. Scanner.cam exports image PDFs and does not currently offer OCR text export — if you need editable text from a scan, that is a genuine reason to pick an app with OCR.
  • ID and book modes: CamScanner has dedicated modes for ID cards and books. Scanner.cam handles cards and pages with its standard capture, but has no dedicated ID-card mode.
  • Editing and signing: CamScanner offers annotation, e-signatures, and PDF merge and split, some behind Premium. Scanner.cam focuses on capture and export; heavy editing belongs in another tool.
  • Cloud and collaboration: CamScanner syncs to its own cloud and supports shared folders and comments. Scanner.cam has no cloud or sharing features — you download the PDF and send it yourself.

Privacy: where your documents go

This is the sharpest difference between the two. Scanner.cam processes everything on your device — your documents never upload to a server, because there is no server endpoint that accepts them. CamScanner is a cloud product: scans sync to its servers, and its privacy policy states scanned data can be stored in China or other countries, which matters to anyone handling passports, contracts, or financial documents.

CamScanner also has history worth knowing. In 2019, Kaspersky researchers found a Trojan dropper inside a third-party advertising library shipped in CamScanner's Android app; Google removed the app from the Play Store, CamScanner removed the compromised library, and a clean version returned to the store that September. No credible research since has reproduced that finding, and the app remains actively developed. The fair reading is not that CamScanner is unsafe today — it is that a cloud scanner app is a bigger trust decision than a tool that never receives your documents at all.

Which one should you pick?

  • Pick Scanner.cam if: you scan occasionally, you want a clean PDF with no watermark and no account, you are on a borrowed or shared device, or you are scanning sensitive documents you would rather never upload anywhere.
  • Pick CamScanner if: you scan daily, you need OCR text export, you want your scans synced across phone and tablet, or you rely on ID mode, e-signatures, and cloud collaboration — and you accept the account, the ads on the free tier, and the Premium price for the full feature set.
  • Use both if: you keep Scanner.cam bookmarked for quick, private, one-off scans and CamScanner installed for heavy, everyday document work. They are not mutually exclusive.

Either way, the scan itself matters more than the tool: flat pages, even light, all four corners in frame, and a zoom-in check before you send. A careful scan from a free browser tool beats a rushed scan from a paid app every time.

Is Scanner.cam the same as CamScanner?
No. Scanner.cam is an independent, browser-based scanner. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to CamScanner or its developer INTSIG. They are separate products that solve the same problem differently.

Does CamScanner's free version add a watermark?
Yes. Free-tier exports carry a "Scanned by CamScanner" watermark, and removing it requires the Premium subscription. Scanner.cam exports have no watermark and no paid tier.

How much does CamScanner Premium cost?
Around $4.99 a month, depending on the plan and region — weekly, monthly, and annual options exist. Prices change, so check the current listing in your app store before subscribing.

Is CamScanner safe to use today?
The 2019 incident — a Trojan dropper in a third-party ad library — was fixed the same year, and no credible research since has reproduced it. The live consideration is not malware but trust: CamScanner is a cloud product that stores your scans on its servers, while Scanner.cam processes everything on your device.

Can I use Scanner.cam instead of installing CamScanner?
For straightforward scanning — capture, clean up, export a watermark-free PDF — yes, with no install and no account. If you need OCR text export, cloud sync, or advanced editing, CamScanner's feature set still has the edge.

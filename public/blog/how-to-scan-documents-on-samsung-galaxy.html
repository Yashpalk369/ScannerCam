How to Scan Documents on a Samsung Galaxy Phone

By Scanner.cam Team · Updated 19 Oct 2026 · 4 min read
Quick answer

Your Samsung Galaxy phone already has a document scanner built into the stock Camera app — point it at a page, let it detect the edges, and tap to scan. You get a flattened, perspective-corrected image saved straight to your Gallery with no app to install, no account to create, and nothing to pay.

What the built-in Galaxy scanner can do

  • Automatic document detection. Hold the camera over a page and the app draws a yellow border around the detected edges — no manual framing needed.
  • One-tap scanning. A "Scan" or "Tap to scan" prompt appears when a document is detected; one tap captures a cleaned, straightened image.
  • Perspective correction. The scan flattens the page so angled photos look like they were taken square-on, which matters for forms and contracts.
  • Text recognition (the "T" icon). On many Galaxy phones, tapping the small T icon lets you extract printed text from the captured image.
  • Everything stays local. Scans land in your Gallery — no cloud upload, no subscription, no watermark.

Step-by-step: scan with the Camera app

  1. Open the stock Camera app and switch to Photo mode.
  2. If document scanning isn't active, open Camera Settings (the gear icon) and make sure Scene optimizer and Scan documents are enabled. Steps vary slightly by One UI version — on newer phones the toggle lives under Settings → Scanning → Document Scan.
  3. Lay the document flat on a well-lit surface and hold the phone directly above it, filling the frame.
  4. Wait for the yellow border to appear around the page edges — that's the detection locking on.
  5. Tap the Scan (or "Tap to scan") prompt that appears near the shutter area.
  6. Review the result and drag the corner handles to fine-tune the crop if the detection missed an edge.
  7. Tap Save — the scan is stored in your Gallery, ready to share.

Newer Galaxy phones: the dedicated Document Scan mode

On recent Galaxy phones running One UI 8.5, Samsung added a dedicated Document Scan mode inside the Camera settings (Settings → Scanning → Document Scan). Once enabled, you can add it to your app launcher, drop it on the home screen, or long-press the Camera icon to jump straight in. It offers Auto and Manual capture modes, four enhancement filters (Clean, Natural, Grayscale, Black & White), and cleanup options that remove fingers, corner folds, and moiré patterns from the image — plus LED flash control with Off, Auto, On, and Always On for tricky lighting. If your phone runs an older One UI version, the standard Camera scan flow above works just as well for everyday documents.

Get cleaner results from the built-in scanner

  • Light it evenly. Natural daylight near a window beats overhead bulbs — it keeps text contrast high and avoids the Galaxy's noise-reduction softening fine print.
  • Shoot high resolution. In the Document Scan settings on supported phones, set Scan resolution to High — small text survives much better.
  • Keep the background simple. Detection works best when the page edges stand out against a plain, contrasting surface like a dark desk.
  • Hold steady for one second. Auto-capture fires fast, but a brief pause before it triggers gives you the sharpest frame.
  • Retake beats repair. If a corner is blurred or a shadow crosses the text, tap Retake immediately — a fresh capture is always better than editing a flawed one.

Turn Galaxy scans into a clean PDF

The stock Camera scan saves an image, not a PDF — and multi-page documents come out as separate photos. To finish properly:

  1. For a quick PDF: open the scan(s) in your Gallery, use the Share menu, and choose a print-to-PDF option or send them to Google Drive's scanner to compile.
  2. For a private, watermark-free PDF: open Scanner.cam in your Galaxy's browser, capture or upload your pages, crop and clean them, then export one consolidated PDF. Nothing uploads — the whole process runs on your phone, so bank forms and IDs stay private.
  3. Name and file it: "2026-10-17-lease-agreement.pdf" is findable; "scan-004.pdf" is not.

FAQ

Do I need to install an app to scan on a Samsung Galaxy?
No. The stock Camera app includes document detection and scanning on most Galaxy phones running One UI 2.0 or later. Check that "Scan documents" is enabled under Camera Settings → Scene optimizer (or Settings → Scanning on newer phones).

Where do Galaxy document scans save?
In your Gallery app as images. They aren't converted to PDF automatically — use the share-to-PDF route or a browser tool like Scanner.cam to compile pages into one PDF.

Why isn't the "Tap to scan" prompt appearing?
Document detection needs the "Scan documents" toggle enabled, a flat well-lit page, and enough contrast between the page and the surface beneath it. Dark text on white paper over a dark desk works best; glossy or crumpled pages confuse the detection.

Is scanning documents with the Galaxy Camera private?
Yes — the capture and edge detection happen on the phone itself, and scans save to your local Gallery. Nothing is uploaded by the Camera app. Just watch where you share or back up the images afterwards.

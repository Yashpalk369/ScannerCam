How to Scan Documents on a Chromebook With the Camera App

By Scanner.cam Team · Updated 19 Oct 2026 · 4 min read
Quick answer

Your Chromebook has a document scanner built right into the pre-installed Camera app — switch to Scan → Document mode and it detects page edges, straightens the capture, and saves it as a photo or PDF . No scanner hardware, no third-party app, no extra cost.

What the Chromebook Camera scan mode can do

  • Dedicated Scan mode. The Camera app's bottom bar includes a Scan mode with Document and QR Code options — no hidden menus to hunt for.
  • Automatic edge detection. Place all edges of the document within the frame and the app detects and straightens the page, cropping away the desk around it.
  • Save as photo or PDF. Choose Save as photo or Save as PDF — PDF is the right pick for receipts, forms, and anything you'll file or email.
  • Multi-page support. Scan pages one at a time in order — previously scanned pages appear along the side as reference thumbnails.
  • Files land in the Camera folder. Scans save to My Files → Camera in the Files app, ready to upload to Google Drive or attach anywhere.

Step-by-step: scan with the Camera app

  1. Open the Camera app — press the launcher key and search for "camera", or find it in the app launcher.
  2. At the bottom of the window, select Scan, then choose Document.
  3. If your Chromebook has a rear camera, flip to it (camera flip icon) — it's sharper and easier to aim at documents.
  4. Hold the document in front of the camera and align all edges inside the frame, on a background that contrasts with the page (dark desk under white paper).
  5. Scan in a well-lit space, then press the shutter button to capture.
  6. Review the scan — if it clipped a corner, tap Retake; otherwise choose Save as PDF (or Save as photo for single images).
  7. Repeat for additional pages, then open Files → My Files → Camera to find your scans and upload them to Google Drive.

Also in Scan mode: QR codes

The same Scan menu holds a QR Code option — point the camera at a code and the decoded link appears above the frame, ready to open or copy. And you don't even need Scan mode for codes: the Camera app detects QR codes directly from the normal viewfinder.

Get cleaner results from the built-in scanner

  • Use the rear camera when you have one. Front-facing webcams are usually lower resolution — the external/back camera gives noticeably sharper text.
  • Contrasting background. Edge detection locks on when the page stands out from what's behind it — avoid white paper on a white table.
  • Hold it flat and steady. Curling or wrinkled pages defeat the straightening; flatten them first and keep your hands out of the frame.
  • Mind the lighting. Bright, even light beats a single harsh lamp — glare spots and shadows both confuse detection and soften text.
  • Rename after saving. The Camera folder uses system-assigned names — rename scans to something searchable like "2026-10-18 Expense Receipt.pdf".

Turn Chromebook scans into a clean multi-page PDF

The Camera app scans one page at a time, and compiling them can be fiddly. To finish properly:

  1. For a quick PDF: save each page as PDF from the Camera app, then combine them with a PDF merger — or scan each page directly into a connected printer's scan function if your printer supports it.
  2. For a private, watermark-free PDF: open Scanner.cam in Chrome on your Chromebook, capture or upload your pages with the camera, crop and clean them, then export one consolidated PDF. Nothing uploads — the whole process runs on your Chromebook, so sensitive documents stay private.
  3. Upload to Drive: move the finished PDF from the Camera folder into Google Drive so it's safe if the Chromebook is ever powerwashed.

FAQ

Do Chromebooks have a built-in document scanner?
Yes. The pre-installed Camera app includes a Scan mode with Document and QR Code options (document scanning arrived around ChromeOS 96). You need no extra app or hardware — just the Chromebook's camera.

Where do scanned documents save on a Chromebook?
In the Files app under My Files → Camera, as JPEG or PDF files with system-assigned names. Open them from there to rename, upload to Google Drive, or share.

Can I scan multiple pages into one PDF on a Chromebook?
The Camera app scans pages one at a time and shows previous pages as thumbnails for reference. To combine them into a single PDF, merge the saved files with a PDF tool — or use a browser-based scanner like Scanner.cam that exports a consolidated multi-page PDF in one step.

Why won't the Camera app detect my document?
Detection needs all page edges inside the frame, a contrasting background behind the page, and good even lighting. Glossy, curled, or shadowed pages confuse it — flatten the page and fix the lighting first.

Is scanning with the Chromebook Camera private?
The capture and edge detection happen on the Chromebook itself; scans save to your local Files. Nothing is uploaded by the Camera app — just be deliberate about where you move or back them up afterwards.
